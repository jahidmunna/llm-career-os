# Paper Reading Protocol

## Pass 1 — 10 minutes

Read:

- title
- abstract
- figures
- tables
- conclusion

Answer:

> What problem is this paper solving?

## Pass 2 — 30–60 minutes

Read:

- introduction
- method
- experiments

Write:

- previous limitation
- key idea
- mechanism
- evidence
- important assumption

## Pass 3 — implementation

Implement the smallest meaningful version.

Do not reproduce every engineering detail unless necessary.

## Pass 4 — experiment

Change one variable.

Ask:

> Does the central claim still hold under this change?

## Pass 5 — explanation

Write 200–500 words:

- problem
- idea
- implementation
- result
- limitation
- what you would test next

## Paper maturity levels

### Level 1
I can explain the abstract.

### Level 2
I can explain the method.

### Level 3
I implemented the core idea.

### Level 4
I reproduced a meaningful result.

### Level 5
I modified it and measured the effect.
